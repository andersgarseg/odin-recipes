Description

Burgers are a classic and beloved dish that can be customized to suit any taste. These juicy and flavorful burgers are perfect for a quick meal or a fun gathering.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 4 hamburger buns
  • Lettuce, tomato, onion, and pickles for topping
  • Ketchup, mustard, and mayonnaise for serving

Instructions

  1. Preheat a grill or skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. In a large bowl, combine the ground beef, salt, and black pepper. Mix until just combined, being careful not to overwork the meat.
  3. Divide the mixture into 4 equal portions and shape each portion into a patty about 3/4 inch thick.
  4. Place the patties on the preheated grill or skillet and cook for about 4-5 minutes per side, or until they reach your desired level of doneness.
  5. Toast the hamburger buns on the grill or in a toaster until lightly browned.
  6. Assemble the burgers by placing a patty on each bun and adding your favorite toppings and condiments.
  7. Serve immediately and enjoy!
